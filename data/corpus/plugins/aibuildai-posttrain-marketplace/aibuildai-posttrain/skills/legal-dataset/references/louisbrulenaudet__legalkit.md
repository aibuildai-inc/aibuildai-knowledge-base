# louisbrulenaudet/legalkit

53,000 French statute articles, each paired with a question generated by LLaMA-3-70B that the article answers - built for training legal embedding models, usable as article-grounded French SFT.

**louisbrulenaudet/legalkit** "consists of labeled data prepared for training sentence embeddings models in the context of French law" [1]. Each row is an article of a French code (`input` holds the reference, such as "Code civil, art. 265-2"; `output` the article's text) and a `query` written for it [2]. The card describes the labelling: three LLaMA-3-70B instances each generate a query from the same document, a fourth selects the best, and that query labels the article [1]. It lives at https://huggingface.co/datasets/louisbrulenaudet/legalkit .

**Use it for**: retrieval training for French law (query to article), or SFT where the prompt is the `query` and the target is the article or an answer grounded in it. The `start` and `expiration` columns carry each article's validity dates [3].

**Licence**: CC BY 4.0 in the card metadata [4]. The queries come from LLaMA-3-70B [1], whose licence terms apply to its outputs. The one catch: the queries are model output, so check Meta's Llama 3 licence before training on them.

**Shape**: 53,000 rows in one `train` split [5]; six columns [3].

**Hold out**: no split is set aside.

**Origin**: article text from French codes; queries generated by LLaMA-3-70B [1]. Hub API at the check date: `downloads` 208, `downloadsAllTime` 4,482, `likes` 36 [4].

**Trained-on-by**: the Hub's dataset tag lists `IvanDVonga/LegalEmbed` (89 downloads) [6].

**Introduced by**: no paper - the dataset card, crediting Louis Brulé Naudet [1].

## Quality

- Measured duplication: 70 rows (0.13%) repeat another row's `input` and `output`; 79 (0.15%) repeat a `query` [7].
- Validity dates use a far-future sentinel: row 0's `expiration` is "2999-01-01 00:00:00" for an article in force [2]. Filter on it to drop repealed articles.

## Load it

One split; pin the revision:

```python
import datasets

REV = "fe8aeff9e0c838b5f309ee1374c7a277162a985e"  # main at the check date
lk = datasets.load_dataset("louisbrulenaudet/legalkit", revision=REV, split="train")  # 53,000 rows
in_force = lk.filter(lambda r: r["expiration"].startswith("2999"))
```

**Trap**: `input` is the article's citation and `output` its text [2] - the reverse of what the column names suggest for SFT. For query-to-article training the prompt is `query`; for citation-to-text recall it is `input`.
