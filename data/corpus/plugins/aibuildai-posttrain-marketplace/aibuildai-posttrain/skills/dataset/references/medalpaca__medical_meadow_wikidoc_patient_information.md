# medalpaca/medical_meadow_wikidoc_patient_information

5,942 single-turn medical question-answer pairs, extracted directly from the "Patient Information" pages of the WikiDoc medical wiki, one config, one `train` split.

**medalpaca/medical_meadow_wikidoc_patient_information** is one of the constituent datasets of Medical Meadow, the training collection released with "MedAlpaca -- An Open-Source Collection of Medical Conversational AI Models and Training Data" [1], built by the MedAlpaca team from WikiDoc, a collaborative medical-knowledge wiki [2]. WikiDoc has two sub-sites, "Living Textbook" and "Patient Information"; for the Living Textbook the builders used GPT-3.5-Turbo to turn each paragraph heading into a question, but for Patient Information every section subheading is already phrased as a question, so the paper states that rephrasing was "obsolete" for this pool and each row is a direct heading-and-paragraph extraction rather than a model-generated question [1][2]. **The dataset card marks the whole WikiDoc release a work in progress and reports that GPT-3.5-Turbo conversion of the sibling Living Textbook pool produced unsatisfactory results in roughly 30% of cases; that specific caveat concerns the Living Textbook rephrasing step, not this Patient Information pool, whose Q/A pairs are extracted rather than rephrased** [2]. It lives at https://huggingface.co/datasets/medalpaca/medical_meadow_wikidoc_patient_information .

**Use it for**: single-turn instruction-following SFT (the SFT method card) - each row is one `instruction`/`input`/`output` triple with a fixed instruction string, no restriction beyond the general caution the card states about the sibling Living Textbook subset [2]. Maps to a standard instruction-tuning format: `instruction` + `input` concatenated as the prompt, `output` as the target completion.

**Licence**: `license: cc` in the repo's card metadata (a bare Creative Commons tag, no version or variant given) [3]; ungated, public [4]. The catch: no source states which CC variant (e.g. BY, BY-SA, BY-NC) applies, so treat it as an unspecified Creative Commons grant.

**Shape**: 5,942 rows, one config (`default`), one split (`train`), three string columns (`instruction`, `input`, `output`) [5][6].

**Hold out**: nothing found. No source and no upstream trail for this card names an evaluation set this pool might overlap.

**Origin**: built by the medalpaca team (Han, Adams, Bressem et al.) from the WikiDoc "Patient Information" pages; the QA pairs are extracted directly from wiki text, not model-generated, per the paper and card [1][2]. Hub API at the check date: `downloads` 2,854 (30-day), `downloadsAllTime` 20,787, `likes` 32 [4].

**Trained-on-by**: the MedAlpaca 7B and 13B models, fine-tuned from LLaMA on the full Medical Meadow collection ("over 160,000 entries") that this dataset is part of [1]. No source names any other model or recipe trained on this specific sub-file.

**Introduced by**: [1] (Han, Adams, Papaioannou et al., "MedAlpaca"); the dataset's own Hub card lists its paper field as "TBA" and instead points to the paper via the project's GitHub repository [2].

## Quality

- The Q/A pairs are direct extractions of WikiDoc "Patient Information" subheadings (as the question) and their paragraph text (as the answer), with no GPT-3.5-Turbo rephrasing step applied to this pool, per the paper's own account [1] and the README's statement that rephrasing was "obsolete" here because the subheadings are already questions [2].
- The card's ~30%-unsatisfactory-conversion caveat is stated for the sibling Living Textbook subset, whose Q/A pairs the README says were generated by GPT-3.5-Turbo; the README does not state a comparable error rate for this Patient Information pool [2].
- The README calls the wider WikiDoc release "still a WIP" and says the team is "in the process of cleaning" it, without naming what remains to be cleaned in this Patient Information file specifically [2].
- Of the 100 rows read at offset 0 of `train`, every row carries the identical instruction string "Answer this question truthfully" [7].

## Where it came from

Built by the medalpaca team as part of Medical Meadow, the training collection behind the MedAlpaca paper [1]. The source is WikiDoc, a collaborative platform where medical professionals contribute and share up-to-date medical knowledge; its "Patient Information" sub-site structures each article so every section subheading is already phrased as a question, which the builders extracted as the `input` field with the following paragraph as the `output` field, applying the fixed `instruction` "Answer this question truthfully" to every row [1][2]. This differs from the sibling Living Textbook pool, where the builders used GPT-3.5-Turbo to rephrase paragraph headings into questions because the source headings were not already question-shaped [1][2].
