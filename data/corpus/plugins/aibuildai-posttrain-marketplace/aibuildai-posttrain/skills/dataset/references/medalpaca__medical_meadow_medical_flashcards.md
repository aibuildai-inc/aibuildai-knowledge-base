# medalpaca/medical_meadow_medical_flashcards

33,955 single-turn medical question-answer pairs in Alpaca instruction format, machine-rephrased from Anki medical-school flashcards.

**medalpaca/medical_meadow_medical_flashcards** is one of the "Medical Meadow" instruction-tuning datasets released with "MedAlpaca -- An Open-Source Collection of Medical Conversational AI Models and Training Data" [1]. The source material is the Anki Medical Curriculum flashcard deck, a resource medical students and graduates maintain and update, covering anatomy, physiology, pathology, pharmacology, and related subjects; after removing cards that contained images, the authors used OpenAI's GPT-3.5-turbo to rephrase each flashcard's front and back into a coherent question-answer pair [1][2]. It lives at https://huggingface.co/datasets/medalpaca/medical_meadow_medical_flashcards .

**Use it for**: single-turn instruction SFT - the `instruction`/`input`/`output` columns are the Alpaca instruction-tuning triplet, mapping directly to the SFT method card's format (`instruction` + `input` concatenated as the prompt, `output` as the target). No usage-shape restriction is stated by the dataset card or the paper [1][2].

**Licence**: the dataset card declares `license: cc` in its front matter, an unversioned Creative Commons tag with no variant (no BY, BY-SA, etc. specified) and no further licence discussion in the card body; the repository is ungated and public [2][3].

**Shape**: 33,955 rows, one split (`train`), one config (`default`), three string columns (`instruction`, `input`, `output`) [4][5].

**Hold out**: nothing. No source - the dataset card, the paper, or the screening note - flags an evaluation-set overlap or contamination risk for this dataset [1][2][6].

**Origin**: built by the medalpaca team (Bressem, Papaioannou, and coauthors); question-answer pairs are machine-generated by GPT-3.5-turbo from human-authored (medical-student) flashcard content, so origin is mixed human/model. As of the check date the Hub API reports 9,465 downloads (120,158 all-time) and 49 likes [1][2][3].

**Trained-on-by**: all 33,955 rows were used to train the MedAlpaca 7b, 13b, and 30b-LoRA models; the project's own README shows MedAlpaca 13b scoring 0.473/0.477/0.602 on USMLE Self-Assessment Steps 1/2/3, against 0.222/0.248/0.276 for a LLaMA 13b baseline and 0.319/0.312/0.301 for a naive Alpaca 13b baseline trained without this medical data [7].

**Introduced by**: [1] (MedAlpaca paper); the dataset's own Hub card lists its paper field as "TBA" and predates the arXiv posting [2].

## Quality

- The `instruction` field is a fixed string, "Answer this question truthfully", in every one of the 100 rows served at offset 0 of `train`; no other value was observed in that sample [8].
- The paper states cards containing images were excluded before rephrasing, and that the resulting questions and answers are short and narrow in scope because the source flashcards leave little room for extended detail [2].
- No source states a measured error rate, duplicate rate, or human quality-check on the GPT-3.5-turbo rephrasing step for this dataset specifically; none is invented here. The paper's general discussion warns that language models can produce fluent-sounding text that is nonetheless factually wrong, a caution it raises about model outputs in medicine generally, not a measurement of this dataset [1].

## Where it came from

The medalpaca team built this dataset from the Anki Medical Curriculum flashcard deck, a set of cards created and updated by medical students covering the standard medical-school curriculum [2]. Cards containing images were removed because the resulting models are not multimodal [7]; the authors then used OpenAI's GPT-3.5-turbo to restructure each remaining flashcard's front and back into a coherent, contextually relevant question-answer pair [1][2]. The dataset is one of several "Medical Meadow" collections assembled for this work, alongside reformatted Stack Exchange, WikiDoc, and USMLE-derived resources [1].
