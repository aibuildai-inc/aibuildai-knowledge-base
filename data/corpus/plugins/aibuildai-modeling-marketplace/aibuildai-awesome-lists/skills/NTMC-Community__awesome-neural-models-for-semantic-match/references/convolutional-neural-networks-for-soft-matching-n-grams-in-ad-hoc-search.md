# Convolutional Neural Networks for Soft-Matching N-Grams in Ad-hoc Search.

**Source**: [http://www.cs.cmu.edu/~zhuyund/papers/WSDM_2018_Dai.pdf](http://www.cs.cmu.edu/~zhuyund/papers/WSDM_2018_Dai.pdf)

**Code**: [https://github.com/NTMC-Community/MatchZoo/blob/master/matchzoo/models/conv_knrm.py](https://github.com/NTMC-Community/MatchZoo/blob/master/matchzoo/models/conv_knrm.py)

**Year**: 2018 | **Venue**: WSDM 2018 | **Category**: Ad-hoc Information Retrieval

## Description

CONV-KNRM uses convolutiona neural networks to represent n-grams of various lengths and soft-matches them in a unified embedding space. The n-gram soft matches are then utilized by the kernel pooling and learning-to-rank layers to generate the final ranking score.
