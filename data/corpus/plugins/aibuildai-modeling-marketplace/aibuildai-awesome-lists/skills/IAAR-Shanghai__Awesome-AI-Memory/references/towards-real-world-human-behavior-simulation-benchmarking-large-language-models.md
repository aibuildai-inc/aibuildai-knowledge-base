# Towards Real-world Human Behavior Simulation: Benchmarking Large Language Models on Long-horizon, Cross-scenario, Heterogeneous Behavior Traces

**Source**: [https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.08362](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.08362)

**Category**: Datasets & Benchmark

## Description

• Introduces OmniBehavior, the first benchmark built entirely from real-world data for long-horizon, cross-scenario, heterogeneous behavior simulation. • Highlights that real user decisions depend on cross-scenario causal chains that are largely missing from prior datasets. • Shows that current LLMs remain weak on complex behavior simulation even with longer contexts, and exhibit homogenized personalities and optimistic bias.
