# Unlearn What You Want to Forget: Efficient Unlearning for LLMs

**Source**: [https://aclanthology.org/anthology-files/pdf/emnlp/2023.emnlp-main.738.pdf](https://aclanthology.org/anthology-files/pdf/emnlp/2023.emnlp-main.738.pdf)

**Category**: Framework & Methods

## Description

• Introduces the Efficient Unlearning (EUL) framework, designed to address the challenges of handling user privacy data in large language models (LLMs). • As LLMs are widely deployed, models may inadvertently memorize sensitive information during pretraining, raising significant privacy concerns. • EUL enables the effective removal of specific sensitive data from LLMs without full retraining, while preserving overall predictive performance.
