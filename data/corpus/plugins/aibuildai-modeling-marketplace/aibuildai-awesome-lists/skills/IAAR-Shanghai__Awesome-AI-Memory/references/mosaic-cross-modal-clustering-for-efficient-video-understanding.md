# Mosaic: Cross-Modal Clustering for Efficient Video Understanding

**Source**: [https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.10060](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.10060)

**Category**: Systems & Models

## Description

• Proposes Mosaic, a cross-modal clustering system for efficient long-video understanding. • Finds implicit clustering structure in VLM KV caches and uses clusters as the unit for cache organization, maintenance, and retrieval. • Reduces fragmented transfers and management cost, achieving up to 1.38x speedups in streaming video inference.
