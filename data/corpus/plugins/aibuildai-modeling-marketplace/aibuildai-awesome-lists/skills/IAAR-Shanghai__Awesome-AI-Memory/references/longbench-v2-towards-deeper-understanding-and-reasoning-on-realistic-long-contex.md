# LongBench v2: Towards Deeper Understanding and Reasoning on Realistic Long-context Multitasks

**Source**: [https://arxiv.org/pdf/2412.15204](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2412.15204)

**Category**: Datasets & Benchmark

## Description

• LongBench v2 is a multi-task benchmark for evaluating large language models (LLMs) in long-context understanding and reasoning. • It consists of 503 multiple-choice questions spanning diverse task types, with a focus on comprehending and answering long-form text. • The study finds that the best-performing models surpass human experts in long-context tasks, highlighting the importance of enhanced reasoning and increased test-time computation.
