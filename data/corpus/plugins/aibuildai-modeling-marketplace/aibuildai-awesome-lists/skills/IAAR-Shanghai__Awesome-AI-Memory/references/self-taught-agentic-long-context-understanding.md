# Self-Taught Agentic Long-Context Understanding

**Source**: [https://arxiv.org/pdf/2502.15920](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2502.15920)

**Category**: Datasets & Benchmark

## Description

• The Agentic Long-Context Understanding (AgenticLU) framework is designed to enhance large language models (LLMs) in long-text understanding and reasoning. • AgenticLU introduces a Chain-of-Clarifications (CoC) mechanism that optimizes the model’s self-clarification process and employs tree-structured search paths to generate clarification questions, thereby significantly improving the accuracy and effectiveness of multi-step reasoning. • Evaluation results show that the framework outperforms existing prompting techniques on long-context question answering, while keeping computational overhead well controlled.
