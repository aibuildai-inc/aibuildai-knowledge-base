# Towards Effective Evaluations and Comparisons for LLM Unlearning Methods

**Source**: [https://arxiv.org/pdf/2406.09179](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2406.09179)

**Category**: Datasets & Benchmark

## Description

• Explores machine unlearning in large language models (LLMs) and the importance of its evaluation, with a particular focus on eliminating unnecessary data memorization. • The study addresses two key challenges: the robustness of evaluation metrics and the trade-off between removing target knowledge and preserving other knowledge. • It recommends Extraction Strength (ES) as a primary evaluation metric to ensure accuracy and robustness in unlearning assessment.
