# PersonaBench: Evaluating AI Models on Understanding Personal Information through Accessing (Synthetic) Private User Data

**Source**: [https://aclanthology.org/2025.findings-acl.49.pdf](https://aclanthology.org/2025.findings-acl.49.pdf)

**Category**: Datasets & Benchmark

## Description

• PersonaBench is a benchmark designed to evaluate AI models’ ability to understand personal information. • The paper highlights the importance of personalization in AI assistants and emphasizes the challenge posed by the lack of publicly available datasets for assessing such capabilities. • The evaluation primarily focuses on retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) models, with results indicating that current models still struggle to effectively handle personal queries.
