# According to Me: Long-Term Personalized Referential Memory QA

**Source**: [https://arxiv.org/pdf/2603.01990](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2603.01990)

**Category**: Datasets & Benchmark

## Description

• Traditional benchmarks are mostly based on single-text dialogue histories and therefore cannot effectively evaluate agents' personalized referential reasoning ability in real-life, multimodal, and long-horizon settings. • Introduces ATM-Bench, a multimodal and multi-source benchmark built from four years of private data, and proposes schema-guided memory for structured representation of heterogeneous data. • Reveals the performance limitations of existing systems on complex real-world experience collections, and shows that structured SGM outperforms traditional descriptive methods in multi-source scenarios.
