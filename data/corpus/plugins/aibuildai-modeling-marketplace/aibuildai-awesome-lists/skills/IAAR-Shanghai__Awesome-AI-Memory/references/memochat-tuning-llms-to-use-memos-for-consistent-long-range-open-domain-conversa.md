# MemoChat: Tuning LLMs to Use Memos for Consistent Long-Range Open-Domain Conversation

**Source**: [https://arxiv.org/pdf/2308.08239](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2308.08239)

**Category**: Framework & Methods

## Description

• Proposes MemoChat, an instruction tuning pipeline designed to enable Large Language Models (LLMs) to employ self-composed memos for maintaining consistency in long-range open-domain conversations. • The approach utilizes a "memorization-retrieval-response" cycle, teaching LLMs to restructure dialogue history into memos and retrieve relevant evidence for answering current queries. • Experiments show that MemoChat outperforms strong baselines on a newly curated, expert-annotated consistency benchmark (MT-Bench+), verifying the efficacy of the memo-equipped inner thinking process.
