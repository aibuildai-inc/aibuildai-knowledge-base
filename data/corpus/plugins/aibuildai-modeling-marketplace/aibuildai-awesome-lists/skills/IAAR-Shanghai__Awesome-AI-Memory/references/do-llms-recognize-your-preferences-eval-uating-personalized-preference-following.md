# DO LLMS RECOGNIZE YOUR PREFERENCES? EVAL-UATING PERSONALIZED PREFERENCE FOLLOWING IN LLMS

**Source**: [https://arxiv.org/pdf/2502.09597](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2502.09597)

**Category**: Datasets & Benchmark

## Description

• PREFEVAL is a benchmark designed to evaluate large language models (LLMs) in their ability to infer, remember, and follow user preferences over long conversations. • The benchmark includes 3,000 user preference–query pairs spanning 20 topics, revealing significant challenges for current LLMs in adhering to user preferences. • The study shows that explicit preferences are easier for models to infer than implicit preferences, and that task types and preference expression styles have a substantial impact on model performance.
