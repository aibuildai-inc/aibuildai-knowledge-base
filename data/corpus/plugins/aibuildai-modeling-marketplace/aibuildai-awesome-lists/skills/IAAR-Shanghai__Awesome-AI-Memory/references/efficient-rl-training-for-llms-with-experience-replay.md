# Efficient RL Training for LLMs with Experience Replay

**Source**: [https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.08706](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.08706)

**Category**: Framework & Methods

## Description

• Revisits experience replay for LLM post-training and questions the assumption that only fresh on-policy data is useful. • Analyzes the trade-off between staleness, diversity, and generation cost in replay buffer design. • Shows that well-designed replay buffers can cut inference cost substantially without hurting and sometimes even improving performance.
