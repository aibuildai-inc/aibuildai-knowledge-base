# Towards effective evaluation and comparisons for LLM unlearning methods

**Source**: [https://openreview.net/forum?id=aLLuYpn83y](https://openreview.net/forum?id=aLLuYpn83y)

**Category**: Framework & Methods

## Description

• The paper examines machine unlearning in large language models (LLMs) and the importance of its evaluation, with a particular focus on removing undesirable or unnecessary data memories. • It introduces Unlearning with Calibration (UWC) to calibrate model performance and strengthen the evaluation of different unlearning methods. • The study emphasizes the importance of selecting appropriate evaluation metrics and recommends Extraction Strength (ES) as a primary evaluation tool to ensure accuracy and robustness in assessment.
