# A Survey of Machine Unlearning in Large Language Models: Methods, Challenges and Future Directions

**Source**: [https://arxiv.org/pdf/2503.01854v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2503.01854v2)

**Category**: Survey

## Description

• The paper investigates machine unlearning in large language models (LLMs), aiming to effectively remove the influence of undesirable data (e.g., sensitive or illegal information) without full retraining, while preserving overall model utility. • It defines the objectives and paradigms of LLM unlearning and establishes a comprehensive taxonomy. • The paper reviews existing approaches, evaluates their strengths and limitations, and discusses opportunities for future research.
