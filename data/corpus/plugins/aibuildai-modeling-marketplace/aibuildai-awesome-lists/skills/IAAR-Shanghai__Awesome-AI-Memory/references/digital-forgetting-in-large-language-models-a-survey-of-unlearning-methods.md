# Digital Forgetting in Large Language Models: A Survey of Unlearning Methods

**Source**: [https://arxiv.org/pdf/2404.02062](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2404.02062)

**Category**: Survey

## Description

• The paper explores digital forgetting in large language models (LLMs) and corresponding unlearning methods, with a focus on addressing issues related to privacy, copyright, and social ethics. • It analyzes different types of model architectures and training processes, as well as practical approaches to digital forgetting, including data retraining, machine unlearning, and prompt engineering. • By introducing the concept of “forgetting guarantees,” the paper emphasizes effective mechanisms for both exact and approximate forgetting.
