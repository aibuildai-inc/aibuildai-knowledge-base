# Large Language Model Unlearning

**Source**: [https://arxiv.org/pdf/2310.10683](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2310.10683)

**Category**: Framework & Methods

## Description

• Explores methods for implementing “forgetting” or “unlearning” in large language models (LLMs) to eliminate undesired or misaligned behaviors. • By applying a gradient ascent (GA) strategy and introducing a random-output loss, the study demonstrates that unlearning can effectively prevent models from generating harmful responses. • Experimental results show that the GA and GA + Mismatch approaches perform particularly well in reducing content leakage rates.
