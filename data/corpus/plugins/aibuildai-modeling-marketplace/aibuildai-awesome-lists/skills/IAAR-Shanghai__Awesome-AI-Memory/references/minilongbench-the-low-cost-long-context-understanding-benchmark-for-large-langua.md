# MiniLongBench: The Low-cost Long Context Understanding Benchmark for Large Language Models

**Source**: [https://aclanthology.org/2025.acl-long.560.pdf](https://aclanthology.org/2025.acl-long.560.pdf)

**Category**: Datasets & Benchmark

## Description

• MiniLongBench is a low-cost long-text understanding benchmark designed to improve the efficiency and affordability of evaluating large language models (LLMs) on long-context understanding (LCU). • By applying data compression techniques, MiniLongBench significantly reduces the number of evaluation samples while maintaining evaluation consistency, and shows a high correlation with results from the original LongBench benchmark. • Evaluations across multiple task categories demonstrate MiniLongBench’s effectiveness, although further improvements are still needed for summarization and synthesis tasks.
