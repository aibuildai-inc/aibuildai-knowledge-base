# OpenUnlearning:Accelerating LLM unlearning via unified benchmarking of methods and metrics

**Source**: [https://arxiv.org/pdf/2506.12618](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2506.12618)

**Category**: Framework & Methods

## Description

• Introduces the “OpenUnlearning” framework, designed to advance research on unlearning in large language models (LLMs). • OpenUnlearning integrates a wide range of unlearning algorithms and evaluation methods, streamlining the research workflow for studying forgetting. • Through targeted and task-specific evaluations, OpenUnlearning ensures the credibility and robustness of unlearning assessment standards.
