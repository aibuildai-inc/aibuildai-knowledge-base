# Discrete Diffusion Models Knowledge Base

Discrete diffusion models extend the continuous diffusion framework to categorical and discrete data
domains. They encompass approaches using absorbing states, uniform transition matrices, and multinomial
noise schedules for text, protein sequences, and other structured discrete outputs. The field bridges
score-based generative modeling with language modeling through masked diffusion, discrete flow matching,
and ratio-based estimation of data distributions.
