# SPHINX-X: Scaling Data and Parameters for a Family of Multi-modal Large Language Models

**Source**: [https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.05935](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.05935)

**Authors**: Peng Gao, Renrui Zhang, Chris Liu, Longtian Qiu, Siyuan Huang, Weifeng Lin, Shitian Zhao, Shijie Geng, Ziyi Lin, Peng Jin, Kaipeng Zhang, Wenqi Shao, Chao Xu, Conghui He, Junjun He, Hao Shao, Pan Lu, Hongsheng Li, Yu Qiao

## Description

SPHINX-X refines multi-modal large language models by streamlining its architecture to use two visual encoders, CLIP-ConvNeXt and DINOv2, and implementing an efficient single-stage training process for enhanced performance across diverse multi-modal tasks.
