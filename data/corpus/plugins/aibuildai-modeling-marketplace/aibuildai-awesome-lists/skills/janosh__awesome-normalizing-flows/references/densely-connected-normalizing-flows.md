# Densely connected normalizing flows

**Source**: [https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.04627](https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.04627)

## Description

Creates a nested coupling structure to add more expressivity to standard coupling layers. They also utilize slicing/factorization for dimensionality reduction and Nystromer for the coupling layer conditioning network. They achieved SOTA results for normalizing flow models.
