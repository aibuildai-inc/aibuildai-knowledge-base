# SpeakerVid-5M

**Source**: [http://arxiv.org/abs/2507.09862](http://arxiv.org/abs/2507.09862)

## Description

Large-scale dataset with 5.2M video clips (8,743 hours) for audio-visual dyadic interactive virtual human generation, covering monadic talking, listening, and dyadic conversations, with pre-training and SFT subsets.
