# TalkCuts

**Source**: [http://arxiv.org/abs/2510.07249](http://arxiv.org/abs/2510.07249)

**Year**: 2025 | **Venue**: ArXiv 2025 | **Category**: Datasets

## Description

A large-scale dataset with 164k clips totaling over 500 hours of human speech videos featuring diverse camera shots and detailed annotations including textual descriptions, 2D keypoints, and 3D SMPL-X motions for multi-shot speech video generation.
