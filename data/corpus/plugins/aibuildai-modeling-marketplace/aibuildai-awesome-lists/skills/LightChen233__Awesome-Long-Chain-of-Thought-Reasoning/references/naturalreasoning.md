# NaturalReasoning

**Source**: [https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.13124](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.13124)

**Category**: 5.2 Training Datasets | **Subcategory**: 5.2.2 Direct Distillation

## Description

validates through knowledge distillation experiments that NaturalReasoning can effectively extract and transfer reasoning capabilities from powerful teacher models. It is equally effective for unsupervised self-training using external reward models or self-rewarding.
