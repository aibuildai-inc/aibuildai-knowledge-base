# Least-to-Most

**Source**: [https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.10625](https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.10625)

**Category**: 2.3 Extensive Exploration | **Subcategory**: 2.3.3 External Exploration

## Description

Breaks down complex problems into simpler subproblems, solving each and using prior answers as inputs for subsequent steps, ultimately synthesizing a solution for the overall problem. This method was proposed to address the "difficulty generalization" bottleneck in traditional Chain-of-Thought approaches.
