## Principle 13: Leverage Domain Knowledge and Competition-Specific Patterns

*Consensus: Liverpool found device noise patterns. Career-Con detected minute bias. Top solutions always do EDA to find domain patterns.*

Every competition has domain-specific patterns that generic ML misses. Market microstructure, physiological rhythms, device artifacts, business rules, physical constraints. Deep EDA and domain research often reveal shortcuts or critical preprocessing steps.

**Why this matters:** Domain insights can be worth more than any model improvement. Liverpool's 50Hz filtering was essential. Sleep's minute bias was a major signal. These aren't discoverable by hyperparameter tuning—they require understanding the data generation process.

**When to use:** Always start with EDA before modeling. Read provided papers/documentation. For scientific data, understand the measurement process. For business data, understand the decision process. For sensor data, understand the device. Look for: patterns in time-of-day, biases in labels, device artifacts, known physical constraints.

**When NOT to use:** Don't over-index on patterns in public data that might not hold in private data. Don't assume domain knowledge from other competitions transfers—each is unique. Don't spend weeks on domain research if simple models haven't been tried yet—start with baseline.

**How to reason about it:**
EDA checklist: (1) Plot target over time—any patterns? (2) Plot feature distributions—any strange artifacts? (3) Check for periodicity (FFT for signals, autocorrelation for forecasts). (4) Read forum discussions—what have others found? (5) If scientific, read the paper—what's the data generation process? (6) Check for hidden structure (batches, groups, experimental conditions). Build domain insights into features or constraints.
