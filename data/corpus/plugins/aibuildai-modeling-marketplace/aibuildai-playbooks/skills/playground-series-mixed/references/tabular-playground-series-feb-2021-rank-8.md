# Congratulations and Thank You

Competition: tabular-playground-series-feb-2021
Rank: #8
Source: https://www.kaggle.com/c/tabular-playground-series-feb-2021/discussion/222748

I wish to thank Kaggle for organizing this competition. A lot of us have been starved for Tabular Data competitions, and this series helps alleviate that hunger a bit.

The fun thing about playground competitions is that you get to do things for fun, and not stress out too much about the standings, medals, points, etc. I was mostly serious about this competition in the first half of February, but then I slackened off, and just casually followed it. Decided to just blend my own best solution with top notebook today, just out of a whim, and to my surprise it was good enough to land me in top 10!

Want to thank and congratulate all the great notebooks that have been shared in this competition. There truly were some new interesting tricks for the tabular-like datasets. I really want to try to apply them to other problems now. 

Special congratulations and thanks goes out to @ryanzhang. His winning solution is just incredible and really impressive. I'll try to put some time into trying to study it and understand it more now.
