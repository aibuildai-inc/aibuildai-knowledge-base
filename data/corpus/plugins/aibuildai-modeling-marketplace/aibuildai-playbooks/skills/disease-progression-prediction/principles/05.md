## Principle 5: Trust CV Over Public Leaderboard

*Consensus: 3rd CMI: 'focused entirely on CV after LB correlation broke'*

Public LB in medical competitions often has very few samples and different characteristics than private test (earlier collection date, different severity distribution). Optimizing for public LB produces 200+ rank drops.

**Why this matters:** Public LB is too small to be statistically meaningful and may have systematic differences from private.

**When to use:** When public LB contradicts your CV. When public/private split is temporal or uses different patient cohorts. When public LB has <50 samples.

**When NOT to use:** When your CV shows no correlation with any submission (this means your CV is wrong, not that LB is wrong). When public LB has 1000+ samples and matches train distribution.

**How to reason about it:**
If your best CV model scores worse on public LB than a weaker CV model, check: (1) is public LB size <100? (2) do train/test come from different time periods or sites? If yes to either, trust CV. Select final submissions by CV rank, not LB rank. Use LB only to verify you're not completely broken.

```python
# Document CV scores for each model
models_cv = [
    ('model_a', 0.450),  # best CV
    ('model_b', 0.455),  # 2nd CV
    ('model_c', 0.460),  # 3rd CV
]

models_lb = [
    ('model_c', 0.440),  # best LB but worst CV
    ('model_b', 0.445),
    ('model_a', 0.450),  # worst LB but best CV
]

# Select by CV, not LB
submit_final = 'model_a'  # CV winner
```
