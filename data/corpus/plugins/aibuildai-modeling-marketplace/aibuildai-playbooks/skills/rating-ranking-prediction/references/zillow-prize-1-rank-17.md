# 17th Place Solution

Competition: zillow-prize-1
Rank: #17
Source: https://www.kaggle.com/c/zillow-prize-1/discussion/47434

I found this to be a really enjoyable first competition, which I tackled with my [brother, Dash][1].  Dash, in addition to being great to work with, introduced me to Machine Learning, Kaggle, and taught me the basics of Python.

While we tried out several different approaches, there were only a few particularly important components to our final solution:

 - Using information about neighbouring properties, including the ~3M observations not associated with a sale.

 - Imposing constraints on the fitting in order to achieve more robust results.

 - Stacking

If you are interested in a bit more detail, please see the attached file for a PowerPoint presentation (given at the Vancouver [Learn Data Science][2] Meetup).  I would also be happy to answer any questions.

Thank-you to Kaggle for being a forum for this kind of competition, and to Zillow for sponsoring!
