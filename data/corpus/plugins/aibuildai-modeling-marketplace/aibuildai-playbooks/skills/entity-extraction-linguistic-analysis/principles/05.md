## Principle 5: Ensemble for Diversity in Architecture, Not Just Seeds

*Consensus: Recurring across competitions - architectural diversity emphasized in most top solutions*

Build ensemble diversity through different model architectures (RoBERTa vs DeBERTa vs BART), prediction heads (linear vs LSTM/GRU head), max_length, and training data subsets—not just random seeds of the same model.

**Why this matters:** Models with identical architecture but different seeds make correlated errors. Diversity in architecture, input length, or prediction mechanism captures different error modes, leading to better ensemble performance. A model that is weaker alone can still improve the blend when its errors are decorrelated from the stronger models.

**When to use:** Always ensemble diverse models for final submission. Prioritize: (1) different backbones (DeBERTa-v3-large + DeBERTa-large + RoBERTa-large > 3x DeBERTa-v3-large), (2) different max_length (1536 + 512 + 128 captures short and long context), (3) different heads (linear token classifier + LSTM/GRU head), (4) different training tricks (pseudo-labels + adversarial + vanilla). Then add 3-5 seeds per configuration.

**When NOT to use:** Skip architectural diversity if: (1) one architecture dominates by >0.02 metric (unlikely), (2) inference time is critical and you can't run multiple large models, (3) you're ensembling >10 models (diminishing returns, focus on the best diverse 5-7). For very large ensembles (>15 models), diversity matters less as errors average out.

**How to reason about it:**
Train 3 architectures (e.g., DeBERTa-v3-large, DeBERTa-large, RoBERTa-large). For each, vary max_length (512, 1024, 2048) if the task has long documents. Train 3-5 seeds per config. Check correlation: compute pairwise Jaccard/F1 between model predictions on validation set. Keep models with correlation <0.85. Ensemble via weighted voting (tune weights on CV using Optuna) or soft averaging of logits. PII's 5th place explicitly varied max_length across team members for diversity.
