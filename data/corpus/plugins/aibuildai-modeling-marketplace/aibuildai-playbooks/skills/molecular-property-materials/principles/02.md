## Principle 2: Preserve Chemical Information at the Representation Boundary

*Consensus: 8/12 polymer writeups used fingerprints or descriptors that preserved chemical information at the representation boundary.*

Treat preprocessing as part of the model: retain the connectivity, repeat-unit attachment points, ring and stereochemical cues, and 3D shape that determine the target. A larger network cannot recover chemistry discarded by an invalid parse, a failed conformer, an over-truncated fingerprint, or a lossy pooling operation.

**Why this matters:** Materials targets often depend on small local differences embedded in global structure. Plain pooling can hide the substructure that drives a property; canonical string handling can lose polymer attachment-point context; hydrogen handling changes what a graph model attends to; and failed conformer generation can silently select a biased subset.

**When to use:** Use whenever raw inputs are converted into graphs, conformers, fingerprints, descriptors, or tokens. Audit parse failures and compare several information-preserving representations before model scaling.

**When NOT to use:** Do not accumulate every available descriptor blindly. Redundant descriptors can overfit sparse targets, and some representations are expensive or fail on part of the data; top polymer solutions used per-target feature selection, compact fingerprint subsets, or fallback models for molecules a representation could not handle.

**How to reason about it:**
Create a representation audit: measure parse and conformer failure rates, atom and attachment-point counts after conversion, ring and stereocenter preservation, and collision rates in hashed fingerprints. Prefer the cheapest representation that preserves the failure-critical information.

```python
def representation_audit(raw, encoded, conformer=None):
    return {
        "parse_ok": encoded is not None,
        "atom_count_preserved": encoded.num_atoms == raw.num_atoms,
        "attachment_points_preserved": encoded.num_wildcards == raw.num_wildcards,
        "stereo_preserved": encoded.num_stereocenters == raw.num_stereocenters,
        "conformer_ok": conformer is not None,
    }
```
