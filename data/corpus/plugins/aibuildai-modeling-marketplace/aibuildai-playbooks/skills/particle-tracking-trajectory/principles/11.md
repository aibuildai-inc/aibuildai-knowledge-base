## Principle 11: Recover Missing and Duplicate Hits Only After a High-Purity Core Exists

*Consensus: 4/6 explicitly used track prolongation or supervised/geometric extension; the winner added same-layer duplicate hits in a separate late stage.*

First establish a high-confidence trajectory core, then extrapolate to expected surfaces to add missing measurements, resolve duplicates, and optionally refit. Use stricter acceptance when an added hit could push the candidate below the metric's purity threshold.

**Why this matters:** Longer cores estimate curvature, direction, and uncertainty more reliably than seeds, so late recovery can search a larger neighborhood without recreating the original combinatorial explosion. It also separates evidence for a trajectory from secondary measurements produced on the same detector surface.

**When to use:** Use extension when clustering or seeding has high precision but misses hits because of narrow bins, gaps, or duplicate sensor measurements. Supervised extension is useful when several nearby hits compete and track context is informative.

**When NOT to use:** Do not extend weak or contaminated seeds, and do not force a hit on every nominal surface when inefficiency or acceptance gaps are common. If the scoring metric rewards purity far more than completeness, leaving ambiguous hits unclaimed or isolated may be preferable.

**How to reason about it:**
For each core, predict which surfaces are physically reachable and derive a local uncertainty region. Compare the expected score gain from coverage against the chance of invalidating purity. Refit after each accepted addition, but prevent drift by retaining a robust core or using outlier-resistant fitting.

```python
for track in high_purity_tracks:
    for surface in reachable_surfaces(track):
        pred, cov = extrapolate(track, surface)
        nearby = spatial_index[surface].query_ellipse(pred, cov)
        hit = rank_extension_candidates(track, nearby)
        if hit is not None and extension_gain(track, hit) > 0:
            track.add(hit)
            track.robust_refit()
    add_same_surface_duplicates(track, stricter_gate=True)
```
