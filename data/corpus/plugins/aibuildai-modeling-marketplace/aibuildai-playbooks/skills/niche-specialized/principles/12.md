## Principle 12: Leverage External Data and Pretraining with Domain Alignment

*Consensus: Critical in the audio competitions (past competitions, Xeno-Canto, AudioSet pretraining), 2/2 LLM challenges (pretrained LLMs fine-tuned), and 1/1 explainability task (patent databases).*

Use external data or pretrained models from the SAME or closely related domain. For audio: past competition data, species databases, environmental recordings. For LLM: pretrained language models. For patents: public patent databases. Domain alignment matters more than data volume.

**Why this matters:** Niche domains have small training sets, making generalization hard. External data provides two benefits: (1) pretraining on large related datasets gives better initialization than ImageNet, (2) augmenting training with domain-matched examples improves robustness. BirdCLEF winners pretrained on past competitions + Xeno-Canto, jumping scores from 0.83-0.84 to 0.86-0.87. But MISALIGNED external data hurts: ConvNeXt (ImageNet pretrained) failed for BirdCLEF 2025 where it worked in 2023, likely due to domain shift. Domain match > volume.

**When to use:** When training data is small (<10k samples per class), when similar tasks/competitions exist with public data, when pretrained models exist for your domain (audio models for audio, bio models for genomics, not ImageNet for everything), or when test distribution is known to differ from training (external data can cover the gap).

**When NOT to use:** When your domain is so niche that no related data exists, when external data introduces label noise or distribution mismatch that hurts more than it helps, or when competition rules prohibit external data.

**How to reason about it:**
Search for: past competitions in this domain, public datasets (Xeno-Canto for birds, patent databases, fraud datasets), pretrained models (Hugging Face, model zoos). CHECK domain alignment: for audio, does external data have similar recording conditions? For text, similar vocabulary? For images, similar imaging modality? Use external data for pretraining OR as augmentation (mixing, pseudo-labeling), not as direct training examples unless labels are reliable.
