## Principle 15: Profile and Optimize Data Loading Bottlenecks

*Consensus: 3/3 Lyft solutions, 1/1 that mentioned rasterizer optimization*

For large-scale trajectory prediction (50M+ samples), CPU-bound data loading (rasterization, augmentation) often limits GPU utilization. Profile the pipeline, vectorize transformations, use batched operations, and move concat/post-processing to GPU. 4×+ speedups are achievable.

**Why this matters:** Training on 100M+ samples takes days even on multi-GPU setups. Lyft: default rasterizer was 0.124s/sample; optimized version was 0.032s/sample (4× faster), enabling full-dataset training in 2 days instead of 8+. This unlocks more experiments and larger models.

**When to use:** Large datasets (>10M samples) with complex preprocessing (rasterization, coordinate transforms, multi-modal fusion). Multi-GPU training where GPUs are idle waiting for data. Any competition where iteration speed is the bottleneck.

**When NOT to use:** Small datasets (<100K samples) load fast enough already. GPU-bound training where data loading is negligible. Simple tabular data with minimal preprocessing.

**How to reason about it:**
Profile with `cProfile` or `torch.utils.bottleneck`. If GPU utilization <80%, data loading is the bottleneck. Common culprits: (1) per-sample loops instead of batched ops, (2) slow coordinate transforms, (3) numpy concat on large arrays, (4) repeated file I/O. Fixes: vectorize transforms (use broadcasting), cache intermediate results (rasters, features) to disk (SSD), move concat to GPU, use multiple DataLoader workers.
