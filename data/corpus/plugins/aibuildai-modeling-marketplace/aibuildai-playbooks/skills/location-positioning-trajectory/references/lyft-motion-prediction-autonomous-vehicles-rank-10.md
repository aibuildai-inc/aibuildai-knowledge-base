In this competition, I tested two papers from waymo & google research:
vector net:   https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.04259   (final solution)
tnt:                https://arxiv.org/abs/2008.08294  (hard to train, = =!)

vector net:
batch=1024, 1 * 1080 GPU + 8 core CPU, trained for 2 days
<=800 vectors





tnt:
batch=128, 1 * 1080 GPU + 8 core CPU, trained for 0.5 day
<=800 vectors
