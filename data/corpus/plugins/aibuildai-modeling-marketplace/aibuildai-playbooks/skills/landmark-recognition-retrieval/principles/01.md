## Principle 1: Select Backbone Based on Pretraining Dataset Scale, Not Just Architecture

*Consensus: Widespread across top solutions 2020-2023. 1st place Universal Embedding explicitly tested this progression.*

The scale and diversity of the pretraining dataset matters more than architecture choice. Models pretrained on LAION-2B (400M-2B image-text pairs) consistently outperform ImageNet-22K models, which outperform ImageNet-1K, even when using the same architecture family.

**Why this matters:** Landmark recognition requires distinguishing between visually similar instances and handling extreme diversity in viewpoints, lighting, and occlusions. Larger pretraining datasets provide richer visual priors that transfer better to fine-grained instance recognition. A ViT-H pretrained on LAION-2B captures more nuanced visual patterns than an EfficientNet-B7 pretrained on ImageNet-1K, regardless of parameter count.

**When to use:** When you have access to models pretrained on large-scale datasets (CLIP from OpenCLIP, models from LAION-2B) and the task involves instance-level recognition with high visual diversity. Universal embedding and multi-domain retrieval tasks benefit most.

**When NOT to use:** When your domain is highly specialized and differs significantly from natural images (medical, satellite), domain-specific pretraining may trump scale. When computational budget is extremely limited, smaller ImageNet-pretrained models may be necessary. Top solutions using only ImageNet pretraining (e.g., landmark 2020 3rd place) still succeeded by focusing on training strategy and post-processing.

**How to reason about it:**
1. Check what pretrained models are available: CLIP/OpenCLIP (LAION-2B) > ImageNet-22K > ImageNet-1K. 2. If multiple LAION models exist, pick the largest that fits your compute (ViT-H/14 > ViT-L/14 > ViT-B/16). 3. For CNNs, EfficientNets and ResNeSt trained on ImageNet-22K are strong baselines. 4. Test whether the pretraining domain matches your task—if the 1st place solution used CLIP, that's a strong signal. 5. If frozen features from CLIP alone score >0.50 on your metric, the pretraining is working; if not, consider domain mismatch.
