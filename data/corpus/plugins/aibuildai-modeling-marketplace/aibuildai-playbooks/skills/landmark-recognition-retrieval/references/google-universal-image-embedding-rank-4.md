Here I would like to highlight my relevant work done with Kerem Turgutlu 
#### [CLIP-Art: Contrastive Pre-training for Fine-Grained Art Classification](https://arxiv.org/abs/2204.14244)

We used 1 year ago many ideas for finetuning VIT pre-trained using CLIP, and even train using CLIP from scratch! so check out the [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2204.14244) and [github](https://github.com/KeremTurgutlu/self_supervised) (+280 stars) if you are interested in this topic :)

>  We are one of the first methods to use CLIP (Contrastive Language-Image Pre-Training) on a great variety of art images and text pairs, being able to learn directly from raw descriptions about images. We aim to solve: instance retrieval and fine-grained artwork recognition.
