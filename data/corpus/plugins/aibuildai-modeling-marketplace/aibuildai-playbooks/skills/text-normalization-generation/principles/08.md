## Principle 8: Extend Inference Max Length Beyond Training Length

*Consensus: A few solutions report gains by using longer max_length at inference than training*

Train with max_length 768 or 1024 (for speed), but at inference increase to 1536 or 2048. This lets the model see full context for long essays without the memory/time cost during training.

**Why this matters:** Long essays get truncated at training length, losing context. The 4th-place summary evaluation solution trained at 850 tokens but inferred at 1500 tokens, improving CV from 0.498 to 0.487. The model generalizes well to longer sequences because positional embeddings extrapolate and the task doesn't change. However, some models degrade—validate on CV before trusting.

**When to use:** Use when: (a) Training data has long texts that exceed your training max_length, (b) You've validated on CV that extending inference length improves scores (check per-fold—improvement may be prompt-specific), (c) Inference time allows (longer sequences = slower inference).

**When NOT to use:** Don't extend if: (a) All texts fit comfortably in training max_length (no truncation), (b) Extending degrades CV (some models don't generalize to longer lengths), (c) Inference time is hard-constrained (e.g., 9-hour limit with many models).

**How to reason about it:**
Check: What % of training samples are truncated at your current max_length? If > 20%, extending at inference may help. Validate by fold: does each fold improve with longer inference length, or just certain prompts (which may not appear in test)? Start conservatively: if training at 1024, try 1280 or 1536 at inference. Beyond 2048, gains plateau and inference slows significantly. Trade-off: longer inference means fewer models in ensemble due to time limits.

# Training: max_length = 1024
train_dataset = tokenize(train_texts, max_length=1024, truncation=True)
model.fit(train_dataset)

# Inference: max_length = 1536
test_dataset = tokenize(test_texts, max_length=1536, truncation=True)
predictions = model.predict(test_dataset)
