## Principle 9: Stabilize Training with Multi-Seed Averaging and Full-Train

*Consensus: Many solutions average several seeds; several use full-train (single model on all data) over k-fold*

Writing assessment models are sensitive to random seed (weight init, batching). Train each model with 3 different seeds and average predictions. Alternatively, use full-train: train single model on 100% of data (no folds) for diversity in ensembles, especially when inference time is limited.

**Why this matters:** Single-seed models have high variance—CV and LB can swing by 0.01 depending on seed. The 1st-place ELL solution used 3-seed averaging for everything, making results predictable. Full-train is faster than 4-fold (1 model vs. 4) and often scores similarly on LB if train data is large enough. The 4th-place summary solution replaced 4-fold with full-train to fit more diverse models in 9-hour inference window.

**When to use:** Use 3-seed averaging when: (a) You have compute for 3x training, (b) Single-seed CV varies by > 0.005 across seeds, (c) You're selecting final submissions and want stable predictions. Use full-train when: (a) Inference time is limited and you want ensemble diversity, (b) Train data > 10k (enough for stable single model), (c) CV-LB correlation is strong (full-train scores close to k-fold on LB).

**When NOT to use:** Skip 3-seed averaging if: (a) Training is very slow and you can only afford 1 run per experiment, (b) Variance across seeds is < 0.002 (model is stable). Avoid full-train if: (a) Train data < 5k (high overfitting risk), (b) CV-LB correlation is weak (can't validate without held-out folds).

**How to reason about it:**
For 3-seed: Train same configuration with seeds [42, 123, 999]. At inference, average their predictions (not their weights—ensemble at prediction level). For full-train: Train on 100% of data for 2-3 epochs, use LB (or prior k-fold CV) to validate. Use full-train to increase ensemble diversity: instead of ensembling 4 folds of 1 config (all similar), ensemble 4 different full-train configs (different pooling, max_length, etc.)—more diversity in same inference time.

# 3-seed averaging
seeds = [42, 123, 999]
predictions = []
for seed in seeds:
    set_seed(seed)
    model.fit(train_data)
    preds = model.predict(test_data)
    predictions.append(preds)
final_pred = np.mean(predictions, axis=0)
