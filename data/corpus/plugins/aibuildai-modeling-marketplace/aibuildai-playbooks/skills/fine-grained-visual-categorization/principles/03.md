## Principle 3: Localize the Region of Interest with Bounding Box Detection Before Classification

*Consensus: Top whale/dolphin solutions (1st and 2nd place Happy Whale with YOLOv5/YOLOX, 3rd place Humpback) crop subjects with custom-trained detectors, and all 5 iWildCam 2021 writeups crop animals with MegaDetector.*

Fine-grained tasks often have the subject occupying a small, variable portion of the image with irrelevant background. Training a detector (YOLOv5, MegaDetector) to crop bounding boxes and training the classifier on crops significantly improves feature focus.

**Why this matters:** Background clutter dilutes discriminative features. A 1024x1024 image of a whale where the dorsal fin is 200x200 wastes 96% of the pixels. Cropping forces the model to use texture, markings, and shape rather than context cues (ocean color, boat presence) that do not generalize.

**When to use:** Use when: (1) the subject is a distinct object (animal, product, plant) that varies in position/scale, (2) you have a few hundred labeled bounding boxes or can use a pretrained detector (MegaDetectorV4 for wildlife, general object detectors fine-tuned), (3) background varies significantly between train and test.

**When NOT to use:** Skip for whole-image tasks (satellite imagery, scene classification). Skip when the subject fills >80% of the frame already (a product photo where the item fills the frame). Skip if you lack bounding box labels and cannot manually label even 500-1000 samples for detector training (the ROI for labeling is high).

**How to reason about it:**
Manually inspect 20-30 images. If the subject occupies <50% of the image on average, you need cropping. Train a detector on 500-2000 labeled boxes (for whale dorsal fins, 1st place Happy Whale labeled 5000 bodies). Use the detector to crop ALL training and test images. During training, randomly mix crops with full images at 5-10% ratio for robustness. At inference, ensemble predictions from multiple bbox types (fullbody, backfin) if available.

# Train YOLOv5 on custom annotations
# yolov5/train.py --img 1024 --batch 16 --epochs 50 --data whale.yaml --weights yolov5x.pt

# Crop images using trained detector
from yolov5 import detect
results = model(image_paths)
bboxes = results.xyxy[0]  # x1, y1, x2, y2, conf, class

# Use highest-confidence bbox per image
for img_path, bbox in zip(image_paths, bboxes):
    x1, y1, x2, y2, conf = bbox[:5]
    if conf > 0.4:  # confidence threshold
        crop = Image.open(img_path).crop((x1, y1, x2, y2))
        # Resize crop to square by padding or aspect-ratio resize
        crop = resize_with_padding(crop, target_size=768)
    else:
        # Fallback: use full image or apply center crop
        crop = center_crop(Image.open(img_path), target_size=768)

# Training augmentation: mix bbox types
def random_bbox_mix(image_id, bbox_sources=['fullbody', 'backfin', 'none']):
