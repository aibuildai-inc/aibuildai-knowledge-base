**LB Solution: - The Secret Sauce**

**Phase 1: Selecting Datasets.**

I used the following datasets : 
- DAIGT V2 Train Dataset by @thedrcat
- LLM: Mistral-7B Instruct texts by @carlmcbrideellis
- LLM Extra dataset (Private - generated by me), which I generated from Mistral-7B using the same way that @carlmcbrideellis did.
- Gemini Pro LLM - DAIGT by me (Public)
- Competition dataset.

**Phase 2: Dataset preparation.**
All 15 prompts were kept (at this phase).
The datasets were merged into 3 datasets (Train dataset - all data), (LLM-data only the Mistral data with the Gemini data - all data here with label 1), the original dataset (which 99% human with label 0), named it train_old (Don’t know why !) 

**Phase 3: Training a LogisticRegression for prompt_name classification.**
A simple and quick phase but needed as insurance, why? 
I trained the model to predict each test text prompt_name.
I counted the unique number of prompt_ids ( I know it's 5 but wanted to make it a more general solution).
I selected Top N repeated prmpt_names (where N is the unique number of prompt_ids ) from the 9000+ test set.
In this way, I have for sure the prompt_names in the test set (This can be applied on different datasets also) 

**Phase4: Selecting the Training DataSets**
Now after finding out the prompts used, I selected their crosspondig texts from the Train dataset only. 
I didn’t do this to the LLM-Data set or the old_train (I wanted to keep more general data for less overfitting) 

**Phase5: Spelling Phase**
Added later based on Levenshtein distance for error correction - based on @piotrkoz discussion thread and @wickkey2 posted method ( which saved me some time writing a similar one) 

The error correction was done on All train datasets and test dataset.

**Phase6: The magic of Byte Pair Encoding thanks to @datafan07 golden approach.**
I used vocab size of only 5000 which was enough. 
I created 3 tokenized datasets, Train , test and test+llm_data which is used for fitting.

**Phase 7: Tf-idf** with 3 to 6/7 ngrams range and min_df = 2 (which showed best results) 
**Phase 8: MaxAbsScaler + Ridge/LinearSVR is all you need.**

Until here the public score was around 0.976/7 (with spell checking) and 0.972/3 without and as far as I can tell between 0.894 and 0.905 private

I knew that this was not enough and most likely I would be taking a hit by private LB so I went to digg more. 

**Phase 9: I trained different transformers models** with stemmed words, POS, Lemma, and mixup sentences … I tried a lot but nothing worked well until @mustafakeser4  shared the results over distilroberta.
So I retried with distilroberta I was able to reach 0.915. But in the end, I used the 0.913 version of his work (the 0.927 didn’t show higher results on LB and I selected the weaker model) I didn’t choose mine because it should a slightly lower ensembling results. 

**Phase 10 Ensembling:** I ensemble distilroberta results with my Ridge model results and pushed the score from 0.972 to 0.975 on the public leader board.

**Phase 11 : Secret Sauce and the reason behind my 0.932 and 0.957 private scores.**

Since the test data is somehow manipulated and it's hard to come up with one similar enough. I thought why not train with test! 
I ranked the result for my top ensemble notebook (0.975 back then) and selected X top rows as AI generated (high scores) and bottom Y as human (low scores)  (first exp X and Y was 1000) and added them to training phase and rescored, this pushed the score from 0.975 to 0.982.
I repeated the process 4 times (last exp I repeated it 5 times) each time I increased the X and Y selection by 200/250 rows and I was able to score 0.983 and private 0.922

**Phase 12: More Sauce:**
-This time I selected the median 50 rows from test (rows with scores around the median)
- I ran similarity with train data to find top X (X was set to 10) rows from the train data to those test rows.
- I retrained the Ridge model and updates the 50 rows scores.
- This puhsed public LB to 0.984 and private to 0.929

**Phase 13: I thought that this is not enough and that I need more transformers:**
The data selected from Phase 10 (The Top X and Bottom Y) was used as validation data to distilroberta ( I needed something fast - I am training while submitting !) 
I ran it for a couple of epochs (the best was 4.0) and the best scoring model was sselected. 
I generated new scores which I ensemble by the final results of Phase12 and Phase11, this gave LB score of 0.982 and a private score of 0.957 (without phase 11) and 0.953 with phase 11.

That sumurize my LB solution.
