## Principle 9: Ensemble Models by Averaging Predictions Before Sigmoid/Softmax

*Consensus: Deepfake 41st (average the scores of all networks and frames, then apply the sigmoid); camera ID 2nd and audio tagging 8th (geometric mean of predictions, an average in log space)*

For each model, average its raw logits across TTA repetitions, apply sigmoid/softmax to that average, then average the probabilities across models. Do not apply sigmoid/softmax before averaging within a model's TTA set.

**Why this matters:** Averaging probabilities (after sigmoid) is not the same as averaging logits (before sigmoid) due to the nonlinearity. Logits preserve more information: a logit of -10 and +10 averaged to 0 (probability 0.5 after sigmoid) conveys uncertainty, while probabilities 0.0 and 1.0 averaged to 0.5 lose the certainty of the individual predictions. The deepfake 41st place averaged the raw scores of its 10 networks over all frames of a video and only then computed the sigmoid; the camera model ID 2nd place (27 checkpoints) and audio tagging 8th place (300 predictions per clip) combined predictions with a geometric mean, which is also an average in log space.

**When to use:** Always, unless you have a specific reason to do otherwise. This is the default best practice for ensembling. It's especially important when you have many TTA repetitions or many frames per sample, because averaging logits preserves the signal.

**When NOT to use:** When your models output class probabilities directly (no logits), you're stuck averaging probabilities. Also, if your models are trained with different output ranges (one outputs logits, another probabilities), you'll need to harmonize them first.

**How to reason about it:**
Test both approaches on a validation fold: (1) avg(sigmoid(logits)) vs (2) sigmoid(avg(logits)). The latter is almost always better. For multi-model ensembles, test whether averaging probabilities linearly or via weighted averaging (based on model confidence or CV score) helps. If a train/test distribution shift makes the averaged predictions overconfident, shrink the averaged logit before the sigmoid: the deepfake 2nd place multiplied logits by a constant below 1 (its 'conservative fix') to improve log loss.

```python
# Proper ensembling: average logits before sigmoid, then average probs
import torch

def ensemble_models(models, input_data, tta_transforms):
    """Ensemble multiple models with TTA.
    
    For each model:
      1. Apply TTA transforms
      2. Collect logits
      3. Average logits across TTA
      4. Apply sigmoid
    Then average probabilities across models.
    """
    model_probs = []
    
    for model in models:
        tta_logits = []
        for transform in tta_transforms:  # e.g., 4 rotations
            img_tta = transform(input_data)
            logits = model(img_tta)  # Raw logits, no sigmoid
            logits_inv = inverse_transform(logits, transform)  # Undo TTA
            tta_logits.append(logits_inv)
        
        # Average logits across TTA (NOT probabilities)
        avg_logits = torch.stack(tta_logits).mean(dim=0)
        
        # Now apply sigmoid
        probs = torch.sigmoid(avg_logits)
        model_probs.append(probs)
    
    # Average probabilities across models
    final_prob = torch.stack(model_probs).mean(dim=0)
    return final_prob
```
