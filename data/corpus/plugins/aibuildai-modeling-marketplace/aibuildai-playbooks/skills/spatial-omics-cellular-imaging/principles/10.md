## Principle 10: Augment Acquisition Nuisance, Not Biological Identity

*Consensus: 6/6 families used geometric and intensity augmentation, but top solutions tailored it to orientation, channel physics, stain, scale, organ, or domain.*

Choose augmentations by asking which transformations could occur without changing the biological label. Use them to broaden acquisition conditions while preserving morphology and target semantics.

**Why this matters:** Microscopy often supports dihedral transforms because slide or cell orientation is arbitrary, while stain, illumination, and focus vary across laboratories. Unconstrained augmentation can destroy punctate structures, alter instance masks, or create biologically impossible channel combinations.

**When to use:** Flips and right-angle rotations are usually strong microscopy defaults; calibrated brightness/gamma or per-channel affine jitter helps fluorescence; stain transfer and color jitter help histology; scale augmentation helps variable pixel size; within-organ CutMix can regularize segmentation.

**When NOT to use:** Avoid flips when anatomy has a meaningful axis, channel shuffling when channels identify different markers, severe blur for tiny targets, and cross-organ CutMix when texture-context combinations become impossible. Do not use augmentations solely because they are standard in natural images.

**How to reason about it:**
For each transform, state the nuisance variable it simulates. Inspect transformed examples at the scale of the smallest target. Run leave-domain-out ablations; an augmentation that improves random CV but harms unseen-source CV is probably teaching the wrong invariance.

```python
def build_aug(cfg):
    augs = [RandomRotate90(), HorizontalFlip(), VerticalFlip()]
    if cfg.modality == "fluorescence":
        augs += [PerChannelAffine(scale=0.1, shift=0.1)]
    if cfg.modality == "histology":
        augs += [ValidatedStainAugment(cfg.stain_references)]
    if cfg.variable_pixel_size:
        augs += [PhysicalScaleJitter(cfg.scale_range)]
    return Compose(augs)
```
