## Principle 7: Model Annotation Provenance Instead of Treating All Labels as Truth

*Consensus: 7/9 CAFA writeups explicitly used evidence codes, non-experimental annotations, or filtered GOA/QuickGO records; top solutions repeatedly found these signals valuable.*

Separate experimental targets from computational, curator, literature, and interaction-derived evidence. Encode evidence type, count, relation, and date as features or calibrated priors rather than blindly appending every annotation as ground truth.

**Why this matters:** Non-experimental annotations are noisy but strongly predictive of what will later receive experimental support. Their reliability varies by evidence code, relationship, number of independent supports, and release date; collapsing them loses useful confidence information and can introduce leakage.

**When to use:** Use when external annotation databases expose evidence codes and release history. Preserve an evidence axis for flexible models or aggregate into code-aware counts for trees and linear learners.

**When NOT to use:** Do not use post-cutoff experimental annotations as training features for a prospective benchmark, and exclude obsolete, negated, incompatible, or invalid terms. If provenance cannot be audited, prefer sequence-derived evidence to an opaque annotation dump.

**How to reason about it:**
Create an evidence policy before downloading the latest database release. For each code, estimate conversion to future experimental support on historical snapshots. Check whether multiple independent pieces of evidence improve reliability and whether the feature remains available at the prediction cutoff.

```python
def build_evidence_features(records, cutoff, valid_terms):
    feats = {}
    for r in records:
        if r.date > cutoff or r.term not in valid_terms or r.negated:
            continue
        key = (r.protein, r.term)
        f = feats.setdefault(key, {"count": 0})
        f["count"] += 1
        f[f"code_{r.evidence_code}"] = 1
        f[f"relation_{r.relation}"] = 1
    return feats
```
