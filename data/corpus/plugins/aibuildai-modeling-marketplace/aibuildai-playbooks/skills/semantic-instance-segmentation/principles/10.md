## Principle 10: Train at Lower Resolution, Fine-Tune at Target Resolution

*Consensus: 3/5 competitions - multiple solutions used progressive resolution (Fashion 2nd: 800×1266 → 1024×1600)*

Start training at 0.6-0.8× of target resolution for faster iteration, then continue training from the checkpoint at full resolution for final epochs.

**Why this matters:** Training at high resolution (1600×1200+) is 2-4× slower due to larger feature maps and more memory consumption (smaller batch size). Starting at lower resolution lets you explore hyperparameters and converge the model structure quickly. Fine-tuning at full resolution afterward learns finer-grained details (thin boundaries, small objects) that were blurred at low resolution. Memory savings allow larger batch sizes, stabilizing BN statistics.

**When to use:** When target resolution is >1280 on longest side AND you have limited GPU memory (<16GB per GPU). Also when training time is constrained and you need to iterate quickly. Fashion 2nd place: trained 6-10 epochs at (800,1266), then continued at (1024,1600) - saved ~40% training time.

**When NOT to use:** Skip if target resolution is already moderate (<1024) - the benefit is small. Do not start too low (<0.5× target) - the learned features will not transfer well. Do not use for U-Net if your bottleneck resolution drops too low (verify objects are still visible at bottleneck scale). Skip if you have abundant compute (8× V100) and can train full resolution directly.

**How to reason about it:**
1. Determine max resolution your GPU can handle with acceptable batch size (e.g., batch=2 for 1280×1280 on 11GB GPU)
2. Choose initial resolution:
   - If max is 1280-1600: start at 800-1024
   - If max is 1600-2048: start at 1024-1280
3. Train until validation plateaus (~10-15 epochs typical)
4. Resume from last checkpoint, change only resolution (keep LR schedule continuing, not reset)
5. Fine-tune 5-10 more epochs
6. Alternative: multi-stage (Carvana 11th: 1/4 scale rough outline → full scale refinement)

```python
# Progressive resolution training
# Stage 1: Low resolution
config_stage1 = dict(
    img_scale=(800, 1266),
    epochs=10,
    lr_schedule=[6, 9],  # Drop at epoch 6, 9
)
train(model, config_stage1)

# Stage 2: High resolution - load checkpoint and continue
config_stage2 = dict(
    img_scale=(1024, 1600),
    epochs=15,  # Total epochs, continues from 10
    lr_schedule=[13],  # One more drop
    resume_from='checkpoints/epoch_10.pth',
)
train(model, config_stage2)

# LR typically continues from stage1's final LR (e.g., 0.0002)
```
