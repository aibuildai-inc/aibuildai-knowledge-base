## Principle 8: Ensemble with Test-Time Augmentation at Multiple Stages

*Consensus: 5/5 competitions - all top-3 solutions used TTA, often at proposal/bbox/mask levels separately*

For detection-based models, ensemble augmented inputs (flips, scales) at each stage: Region Proposal Network, bounding box prediction, and mask prediction. Merge via NMS at early stages, average probabilities at mask stage.

**Why this matters:** Each augmentation (flip, scale) produces slightly different detections because features align differently. Ensembling at RPN level (before NMS) gives more diverse proposals - e.g., horizontal flip might detect a partially occluded instance the original missed. Ensembling at bbox level after RPN NMS refines localization. Ensembling masks averages out prediction noise. Open Images 7th place: 12-way ensemble (3 models × 2 scales × 2 flips) at RPN/bbox, then average 12 masks per instance.

**When to use:** Always for detection-based models (Mask R-CNN, HTC) when inference time allows. Especially valuable when: instances are partially occluded (TTA flips reveal different parts), scale variation is high (multi-scale TTA), or you have few models and need diversity (TTA is cheaper than training more models).

**When NOT to use:** Skip for U-Net - just average final predictions (no intermediate stages to ensemble). Skip if inference budget is extremely tight (TTA multiplies cost by num_augmentations). Vertical flip is rarely useful (see augmentation principle). For tracking tasks (NFL), apply TTA to detector only, not tracker (tracking needs consistency across frames).

**How to reason about it:**
1. Minimum: horizontal flip TTA (2x cost, ~+0.005 gain)
2. Better: flip + 2 scales (4-6x cost, ~+0.01-0.02 gain)
3. Best: 3 models × 2 flips × 2-3 scales = 12-18 way ensemble
4. Implementation:
   - Forward pass on each augmentation
   - At RPN: concatenate proposals from all, NMS to merge
   - At bbox: NMS ensemble (if same instance detected in multiple augmentations, keep highest score)
   - At mask: for matched instances across augmentations, average the mask probabilities (after aligning via bbox)
5. Expect diminishing returns: flip (biggest gain), +1 scale (+50% of flip gain), +2nd scale (+25%), +3rd model (+variable)
