## Principle 11: Design Ensembles for Maximum Diversity Across Representation Types

*Consensus: All top solutions used ensembles; most explicitly maximized diversity across model types not just seeds*

Combine models that use fundamentally different molecular representations: SMILES transformers + graph neural networks + tabular (fingerprints/descriptors) + 3D models. This yields much larger ensemble gains than combining multiple transformers with different seeds. Optimize weights by correlation (lower correlation → higher weight) not just by individual performance.

**Why this matters:** Models using the same representation make correlated errors even with different architectures. The MoA 1st-place ensemble (transformers + TabNet + ResNet + DeepInsight CNNs) had correlation 0.63-0.73 between model types vs 0.85+ for same-type models. Lower correlation means more complementary - each model catches different patterns. The ensemble gained 0.002 LB over the best single model despite all singles being strong.

**When to use:** For all molecular prediction tasks when you have budget for multiple models. Prioritize diversity: (1) one SMILES transformer, (2) one graph model (GNN or MPNN), (3) one tabular (XGBoost/LightGBM/AutoGluon on fingerprints), (4) one 3D model (Uni-Mol) if applicable. Then add diversity within each type (different seeds, architectures). Weight by inverse correlation matrix or use Optuna to optimize blend weights on CV.

**When NOT to use:** Do NOT ensemble if you have <3 models (gains are minimal). Do NOT ensemble highly correlated models (>0.95) - you're just averaging noise. Do NOT use equal weights - worse models should get lower weight. Do NOT optimize weights on LB - use CV OOF predictions to avoid overfitting.

**How to reason about it:**
Step 1: Train diverse base models (different representations). Step 2: Compute pairwise correlation matrix on validation predictions for all models. Step 3: Select ensemble members: IF correlation(model_i, model_j) > 0.95 → drop the weaker one. Step 4: Optimize weights: EITHER (a) use correlation-based weighting (lower correlation → higher weight), OR (b) use Optuna/scipy to minimize CV loss with blend weights as parameters. Step 5: VERIFY ensemble beats best single model on CV before submitting.
