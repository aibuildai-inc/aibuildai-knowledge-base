## Principle 4: Apply Multi-Stage Training When Non-Scored Auxiliary Tasks Exist

*Consensus: Several MoA top solutions pretrained on the non-scored targets and transferred to the scored targets; directly predicting scored targets left performance on table*

When the dataset includes auxiliary predictions (non-scored targets, related properties), train a model on auxiliaries first, then use its learned representations to train the scored target. This provides better initialization than random weights and acts as a regularizer.

**Why this matters:** Auxiliary tasks provide supervision signal on the same molecules but different aspects. In MoA, 402 non-scored targets share biochemical mechanisms with 206 scored targets. Multi-stage training in MoA improved from 0.0182 (single-stage) to 0.0156 (3-stage), a 14% gain.

**When to use:** When you have: (1) auxiliary labels on the same molecules (e.g., non-scored MoA targets), (2) related properties that share underlying features, (3) small scored dataset (<2k) but more auxiliary data. Use 2-3 stages: stage 1 trains auxiliary task, stage 2+ finetunes on scored task with lower learning rate.

**When NOT to use:** Do NOT use if auxiliary tasks are uncorrelated with your target (check correlation matrix first). Do NOT use if you only have scored targets. Do NOT train auxiliaries for too many epochs (1-2 epochs max) - you want initialization not overfitting to auxiliaries. The MoA 3rd-place solution found more than 3 stages hurt.

**How to reason about it:**
IF auxiliary labels available: (1) Verify correlation between auxiliary and scored targets (>0.3 for at least some). (2) Stage 1: train on auxiliaries for 1-2 epochs, all auxiliaries together multi-task. (3) Stage 2: freeze backbone (optional), train new head on scored targets for more epochs. OR don't freeze, finetune end-to-end with lower LR. (4) Stage 3 (optional): train only on meta-features = stage-2 predictions as input, new small model on top. CRITICAL: validate on scored target CV throughout, don't overfit to auxiliary task.

```python
# Multi-stage training (MoA-style)
# Stage 1: Train on non-scored targets (auxiliary)
model = NeuralNet(input_dim, hidden_dim, n_auxiliary_targets)
optimizer = Adam(model.parameters(), lr=5e-3)
for epoch in range(2):  # Short pretraining
    train_epoch(model, train_X, auxiliary_y, optimizer)

# Stage 2: Replace head, finetune on scored targets
model.head = nn.Linear(hidden_dim, n_scored_targets)
optimizer = Adam(model.parameters(), lr=1e-3)  # Lower LR
for epoch in range(25):
    train_epoch(model, train_X, scored_y, optimizer)
    
# Stage 3 (optional): Train on predictions as meta-features
stage2_preds_train = model.predict(train_X)  # OOF predictions
stage2_preds_val = model.predict(val_X)
meta_model = LightGBM()  # Smaller model on top
meta_model.fit(stage2_preds_train, scored_y)
```
