# Solution Sharing and Congratulations

Competition: the-nature-conservancy-fisheries-monitoring
Rank: #4
Source: https://www.kaggle.com/c/the-nature-conservancy-fisheries-monitoring/discussion/31538

Big congratulations to our top five winners, especially Paulo Pinto and 24TH for steadily climbing the leaderboard and jumping to #1 and #2 by clear margins.   Private scores are way worse and it's a pretty major shakeup--if anyone has the shakeup metric script comparing to other competitions please run it.  

Our team Chicken of the Sea (#6 private) used an ensemble of ssd with extra data from imagenet, faster-rcnn in caffe, tensorflow, and mxnet, and straight image classification on crops with some standard architectures vgg, resnet, xception, and inception.    See below for more details from Dmytro, Xulei, and me.    We had a really fun team.

Update 1:  Both Paulo's and 24TH's position may be in jeopardy.   Paulo has [openly discussed][1] that his uploaded model does not match his #1 solution, and according to the [old-style leaderboard][2] 24TH has not uploaded one at all.   Combined with a fair bit of [frustration][3], the KDM (Kaggle Drama Metric) for this competition appears to be high.  Stay tuned, and in the meantime, Happy Easter!

Update 2:  After describing the situation to my wife, she immediately responded: "They need to stick to the rules."   Looks like that's exactly what happened.   Congratulations to bobutis and team TROLL, Liuftvafas, clsfishbetter/frankman, x_enhanced_AI, and final medal winners.   It's been a valuable learning experience for all of us.    Please continue to post as you are able--this problem appears to be right on the edge of current CNN capabilities and is a good one to study.
