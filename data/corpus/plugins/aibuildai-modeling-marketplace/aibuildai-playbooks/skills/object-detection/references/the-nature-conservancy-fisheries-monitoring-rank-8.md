# Solution Sharing and Congratulations

Competition: the-nature-conservancy-fisheries-monitoring
Rank: #8
Source: https://www.kaggle.com/c/the-nature-conservancy-fisheries-monitoring/discussion/31538#175817

Congratulation to the winners! This is by no means an easy competition I am sure all of you have spent a lot of time and effort into it. I used many of the methods covered in this thread, and a few of my own. The biggest mistake I've made is not using new boats for validating my classification models until the last few days before stage 1 ends.  I somehow thought the private set would at least retain a portion of existing boats. I even devised a scheme to incorporate "full boat predictions" to the ensemble model as "prior" to smooth out probabilities for uncertain images. For those who are interested, feel free to check out my blog post below which includes a detailed account of my approaches and thoughts on this competition.
