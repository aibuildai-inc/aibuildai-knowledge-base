## Principle 13: Audit Distribution Shift and Leakage as Part of Model Selection

*Consensus: 11/18 Foursquare writeups explicitly discussed the train-test overlap leak; leading ARC writeups also noted train/evaluation/leaderboard shift and, in one case, exploited public-equals-private feedback.*

Treat unexpectedly strong leaderboard gains, overfit models outperforming sound validation, or exact train-test keys as evidence to investigate—not as proof of genuine reasoning. Maintain a leak-resistant model path and distinguish competition-specific exploitation from transferable methodology.

**Why this matters:** Relational datasets are unusually vulnerable to leakage because multiple rows describe the same entity or template. Leakage can enter through direct duplicates, pair generation, target encodings, pretrained candidate models, graph statistics, or replacement datasets used only during private inference.

**When to use:** Run leakage audits whenever records share latent entities, synthetic generators, sources, coordinates, or near-duplicate text, and whenever CV/leaderboard correlation changes sharply. Verify what files and statistics will actually be available in the rerun environment.

**When NOT to use:** Do not assume every CV/LB gap is leakage; candidate-universe size, geographic mix, or label noise may also cause shift. Investigate with controlled ablations rather than optimizing against speculation.

**How to reason about it:**
Search for exact and fuzzy overlap using non-target keys; compare group-disjoint, source-disjoint, and temporal/geographic validation; test whether increased epochs improve leaderboard while harming clean CV; and produce scores with and without suspect features. Favor the clean path unless explicit competition rules make overlap a legitimate deployment condition.

```python
audit = {
    "exact_overlap": overlap_rate(train, test, keys=safe_identity_keys),
    "group_cv": evaluate(group_disjoint_split),
    "source_cv": evaluate(source_disjoint_split),
    "suspect_ablation": compare(with_suspect_features, without_suspect_features),
}
if audit_indicates_leakage(audit):
    final_model = leak_resistant_pipeline
    document_nontransferable_variant_separately()
```
