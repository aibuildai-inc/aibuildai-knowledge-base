## Principle 8: Regularize Deeply with High Dropout, AWP, and Focal/Weighted Loss to Enable Long Training

*Consensus: 6/7 competitions used aggressive regularization. ASL signs 1st: dropout 0.8, AWP, drop_path 0.2. ASL fingerspelling 1st: 400 epochs. MABe 5th: dropout up to 0.35.*

To train from scratch or fine-tune for 300+ epochs without overfitting, combine high dropout rates (0.2-0.8 depending on position), adversarial weight perturbation (AWP), drop path, and class-balanced losses (focal loss, weighted CE) to force the model to learn robust features.

**Why this matters:** Deep models trained for many epochs on limited data overfit without strong regularization. Longer training (300-400 epochs) is critical for achieving top scores because it allows the model to learn fine-grained patterns, but requires regularization to generalize. Dropout prevents co-adaptation, AWP smooths the loss landscape, focal/weighted loss addresses class imbalance. Combined, they allow deeper and wider models.

**When to use:** Always when training > 100 epochs or using deep models (> 8 layers). Apply: (1) Dropout at multiple positions: low (0.1-0.2) within blocks, high (0.5-0.8) before the final classifier. (2) Drop path (stochastic depth) for residual architectures (0.1-0.3). (3) AWP after warmup (start at epoch 0.1-0.3 of total, adv_lr ~ 0.1-0.2). (4) Focal loss (gamma=2.0) for imbalanced classification, or weighted CE with per-class weights. (5) Label smoothing (0.1-0.25) for classification.

**When NOT to use:** Do not apply high dropout everywhere — too much in early layers or without sufficient capacity causes underfitting. ASL 1st place: applied dropout 0.8 only after GAP, not in backbone. Do not use AWP with FP16 if it causes NaNs (tune adv_lr or skip AWP). Do not use focal loss for balanced tasks or when you need probability calibration (focal loss distorts probabilities).

**How to reason about it:**
Plan to train 300-400 epochs (ASL, MABe top solutions). To prevent overfitting: (1) Start with dropout 0.1-0.2 in the backbone, 0.5-0.8 before the classifier. (2) Add drop_path 0.1-0.3 if using residual blocks. (3) Check class balance — if imbalanced (common in action detection), use focal loss (gamma=2.0) or weighted CE with weights inversely proportional to class frequency. (4) Add AWP after 10-30% of training (set adv_lr = base_lr * 0.1-0.2). (5) Monitor CV vs LB — if gap widens, increase regularization. (6) Ablate each regularization technique to measure its contribution.
