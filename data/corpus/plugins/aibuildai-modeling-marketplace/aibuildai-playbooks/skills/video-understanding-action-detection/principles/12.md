## Principle 12: Build Ensemble Diversity Through Architecture, Augmentation, and Fold Variation, Not Just Seeds

*Consensus: All top solutions ensembled. ASL fingerspelling 1st: 2 seeds with different training configs. YouTube8M 1st: 13+ diverse models.*

Effective ensembles require diversity across multiple dimensions: architecture (different backbones, step sizes, crop strategies), training (different augmentation sets, different folds), and seeds, not just multi-seed averaging of the same model.

**Why this matters:** Ensembling reduces variance and overfitting, but only if models make different errors. Same architecture + same data + different seeds provides minimal diversity (correlation ~ 0.95). Adding architectural diversity (different backbones, different receptive fields) or data diversity (different folds, different augmentations) creates uncorrelated errors, leading to larger ensemble gains. YouTube8M 1st: 13 models with different architectures (LSTM, NetVLAD, Transformer, Conv, XGBoost).

**When to use:** Always ensemble. Start with 2-4 seed ensemble of your best single model. Add diversity by: (1) Architecture: different backbones (EfficientNet vs ConvNeXt, LSTM vs Transformer), different depths/widths, different step sizes or receptive fields. (2) Training: different folds (if using k-fold, ensemble fold models), different augmentation sets (one with CutMix, one without), different hyperparameters. (3) Modality: if hybrid, ensemble video-only, tracking-only, and fusion models. Weight models by validation performance or equal weights. Average logits (before softmax) for classification, average probabilities for sequences.

**When NOT to use:** Do not ensemble models that are too similar — the gain will be < 0.005 and may not be worth the inference cost. Do not ensemble poor models hoping they average out — weak models drag down the ensemble. Only ensemble models within ~0.01 CV of your best. Do not use median or voting for regression tasks or soft labels; use mean.

**How to reason about it:**
Train your best single model with 3-5 seeds. Measure ensemble gain (typically +0.002-0.005 over single best). To increase diversity: (1) Train a second architecture (different backbone or structure) — check if it's uncorrelated with your first (compare predictions). (2) Train on different folds (if k-fold, ensemble all fold models). (3) Vary augmentation or hyperparameters (one model with high dropout, one with AWP, etc.). (4) Ensemble across modalities (video + tracking separately, then together). Weight by validation score or grid-search weights on a holdout. Expect +0.01-0.03 from a diverse ensemble.

```python
# Ensemble with diversity
models = [
    ConvNeXtLarge(step_size=24, seed=42),
    ConvNeXtBase(step_size=24, seed=43),
    XCLIP(step_size=11, seed=42),
    ConvNeXtPico_TSM(step_size=6, seed=42),
]
weights = [3, 1, 2, 1]  # Based on validation performance

logits_ensemble = sum(w * model(x) for w, model in zip(weights, models)) / sum(weights)
prediction = logits_ensemble.argmax()  # For classification

# For sequence: Average probabilities at each decoding step
for t in range(seq_len):
    logits_t = [model.decode_step(t) for model in models]
    avg_logits = sum(logits_t) / len(logits_t)
    token = avg_logits.argmax()
```
